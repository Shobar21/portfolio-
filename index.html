My story

I studied computer science and specialised in web development. My final year project, Smart Buy, showed me how a real product comes together, from the database to the screen the customer sees.

Today I build websites and web apps for clients in Pakistan and overseas, and I also teach. Teaching helps me explain technical choices in plain language, so you always know what I'm building and why.

In my freelance work I also build Python management systems, visualise data with Seaborn and Matplotlib, design networks in Cisco Packet Tracer, create UML and system diagrams, and work on network security in Python. For databases, I start with an ER model and then build the MySQL database from it. I have also worked on one or two research papers.

Questions clients ask

What kinds of projects do you take?

Websites and online stores, full-stack web apps, Python management systems, data visualisation with Seaborn and Matplotlib, network design and network security projects, MySQL database design from an ER model, and UML and system diagrams such as use case, class and business context diagrams.

How long does a website take?

A simple business website usually takes one to two weeks. Online stores and web apps take longer, depending on the features you need. I give you a clear timeline in the plan.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely and communicate by email, chat or video call, whichever you prefer.

What do I need to get started?

Just a short description of your business and what you want the site to do. Logos, text and photos are helpful, but we can work out the rest together.

Can you fix or redesign my existing website?

Yes. I can repair bugs, speed up a slow site and give it a fresh, modern look.
